Serin organizations support multiple team members with role-based permissions. This guide covers inviting teammates, understanding roles, and managing your organization's membership.

# Invite Members

Organization representatives can invite teammates to join their organization on Serin.

### How to invite

1. Click your **Profile** in the navigation bar.
2. Open the **Organization** tab.
3. Select the organization where you want to invite a new member.
4. Go to the **Members** tab.
5. Click **Invite Member**.
6. Enter the teammate's work email address.
7. Assign a role — **Admin**, **Manager**, or **Member**.
8. Click **Send Invite**.

The invitee will receive an email with a link to join your organization. You can view pending invitations and resend or revoke them from the **Members** tab if needed.

### Assign roles

Roles are assigned **per member**, not as a single shared role for all invites. Choose the role that matches each person's responsibilities before sending the invitation.

# Roles

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Admin">
    Admins have full control over the organization.

    - Configure organization profile and settings
    - Create, edit, and manage jobs
    - Invite and manage all members
    - Invite candidates and review all interview sessions
    - Access analysis, reports, and analytics

    **Typical use:** Founders, HR heads, or platform owners.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Manager">
    Managers oversee hiring for their team or roles.

    - Create and manage jobs for their scope
    - Invite candidates and review interview sessions
    - Invite and manage members within their scope

    **Cannot:** Change organization-wide settings or manage admins.

    **Typical use:** Team leads and hiring managers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Member">
    Members assist with day-to-day hiring operations.

    - Invite candidates to interview
    - View interview sessions, transcripts, and reports

    **Cannot:** Manage users, modify jobs, or change organization settings.

    **Typical use:** Recruiters, coordinators, and interviewers.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Permission summary

| Action | Admin | Manager | Member |
|--------|:-----:|:-------:|:------:|
| Manage organization settings | Yes | No | No |
| Create and edit jobs | Yes | Yes | No |
| Invite candidates | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| View analysis and reports | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Invite and manage members | Yes | Limited | No |
| Remove members | Yes | Limited | No |

For full role details, see [Roles →](/getting-started/roles/#organization-member-roles).

# Managing Members

### Remove members

Admins can remove members from the organization. Removed members lose access to the organization's jobs, candidates, and analysis.

### Change roles

Admins can update a member's role as responsibilities change. Review access carefully when promoting or demoting team members.

### Pending invitations

View outstanding invitations from your organization settings. You can resend or revoke invitations that have not yet been accepted.

# Best Practices

- **Limit Admin access** — Grant Admin role only to organization owners and trusted leaders who need full control.
- **Use Manager for team leads** — Hiring managers who create jobs and review candidates but do not need org-wide settings access.
- **Use Member for coordinators** — Recruiters who invite candidates and review sessions without needing to manage jobs or users.
- **Review access regularly** — Remove members who no longer need access, especially when people leave your organization.

<Warning>
Candidates cannot be added as organization members. Organization membership is for hiring team members only.
</Warning>
