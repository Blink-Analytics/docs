Serin uses a credit-based system so organizations pay for the hiring activities they actually use. This guide explains how credits work and where to find your usage.

# What Are Credits?

Credits represent **Interview Minutes** that your organization purchases and spends on Serin hiring activities. Instead of a flat subscription for unlimited usage, credits are consumed based on the work your team does — primarily the duration of AI interview sessions.

Think of credits as prepaid interview capacity for your organization.

# When Credits Are Used

Credits are consumed when your organization uses Serin hiring features. Common activities include:

### Resume screening

When Serin parses and scores a candidate's resume against job requirements — including bulk screening workflows.

### AI interview

When a candidate completes a Serin interview session. Credits are deducted based on the **actual duration** of the interview, not a fixed amount per session.

### Report generation

When Serin generates a structured interview report after a session completes.

<Note>
Exact credit costs may vary by plan and activity type. Check your organization's dashboard or [serin-ai.com pricing](https://serin-ai.com/#pricing) for current plan details.
</Note>

# Viewing Credits

You can view your organization's credit balance from the profile dropdown in the Serin dashboard.

The credit display shows:

- **Available credits** — Remaining interview minutes
- **Total credits** — Your organization's purchased capacity
- **Usage progress** — A visual indicator of how much has been consumed

If your organization belongs to multiple organizations on Serin, each organization has its own separate credit balance.

# Managing Credits

### Remaining credits

Monitor your available credits before scheduling high-volume hiring activities such as bulk invitations or large interview batches.

### Organization usage

Credits are scoped to the organization, not individual users. All team members draw from the same organization credit pool.

### Running low

If your credits are running low, you may need to purchase additional Interview Minutes before conducting more sessions. Contact Serin or visit your billing settings to add credits.

# FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do unused credits expire?">
    Credit expiration policies depend on your plan. Refer to your organization's billing agreement or the Serin pricing page for current terms.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can credits be shared across organizations?">
    No. Each organization has its own credit balance. Credits cannot be transferred between organizations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are credits used for draft jobs or testing?">
    Credits are consumed when actual hiring activities occur — such as live interview sessions and report generation. Testing your own jobs may use credits depending on your organization's configuration.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can view credit usage?">
    Organization representatives can view the credit balance from the profile dropdown. Billing and purchase actions may be restricted to Admins depending on your plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if we run out of credits?">
    Your organization may be unable to start new interview sessions until additional credits are purchased. Existing completed sessions and reports remain accessible.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
