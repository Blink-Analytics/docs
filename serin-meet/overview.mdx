Serin Meet is the built-in video meeting experience for conversations tied to a job application — a live interview, a follow-up discussion, or any other call between a recruiter and a candidate. Meetings are scheduled directly inside Serin and everyone who needs to join gets a simple link, with no third-party tool required.

## Scheduling a meeting

Meetings are scheduled from a candidate's application. When creating one, you set:

- **Topic** — a short title (defaults to the candidate's name and job title, but can be edited).
- **Duration** — anywhere from 5 minutes to 4 hours.
- **Date and time** — shown in your local timezone, clearly labeled.
- **Access** — Public or Private (see below).

Once scheduled, the host and candidate are both notified and the meeting appears on their dashboards with a join link. Additional guests can be invited by email. Meetings can be rescheduled before they start and the updated details are reflected immediately for everyone invited.

## Public vs. Private access

Every meeting is either:

- **Public** — anyone with the link can join. Guests enter their name before joining; no account is required.
- **Private** — only members of the hosting organization and the specific candidate the meeting was created for can join. Everyone else is turned away.

This setting can be changed from inside the meeting at any time and takes effect immediately.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do candidates need a Serin account to join?">
    No. Candidates and other guests can join any Public meeting with just the link — they're asked to enter their name before joining. Private meetings are limited to the hosting organization's members and the specific invited candidate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I reschedule a meeting after it's been created?">
    Yes — as long as the meeting hasn't started yet. Once it's in progress or has ended, the date and time can no longer be changed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I invite people outside the organization?">
    Yes. When scheduling a meeting, you can invite additional guests by email. They receive a join link and can join as guests without a Serin account, as long as the meeting is set to Public.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
