## Before joining

Before entering a meeting, everyone sees a pre-join screen where they can:

- Preview and switch their camera, microphone, and speaker.
- Apply a virtual background or background blur.
- Enter a display name (guests joining without an account are prompted; organization members already have one from their profile).
- See who is already in the call, so no one joins blind.
- See the scheduled time for the meeting.

Two notices are shown before joining:
- A reminder that the call may be recorded for review.
- A heads-up if the current browser may not fully support call recording, with a suggestion to switch to a supported browser.

## Layout and view

Each participant can adjust their own view independently:

- **Grid** — all participant tiles shown at once.
- **Spotlight** — focuses on whoever is speaking or presenting.
- **Sidebar** — main content with a strip of participants alongside.

You can also control how many tiles are visible and hide tiles without active video. These choices only affect your own screen.

## Screen sharing

Any participant with permission can share their screen. While someone is presenting, their screen becomes the main focus for everyone else. Hosts can restrict screen-share permission to specific participants from the host controls panel.

## Chat

Participants can send text messages visible to everyone in the call. Messages are saved as part of the meeting record. Messages sent before someone joined are not retroactively visible to them. Hosts can turn chat off for all non-host participants at any time.

## Reactions and hand-raising

Anyone can send a quick emoji reaction that briefly appears for the whole call — a lightweight way to respond without interrupting whoever is speaking.

Anyone can also raise their hand to signal they'd like to speak. Raised hands are automatically lowered a couple of seconds after that person starts talking. Hosts can lower a specific person's hand, or clear all raised hands at once.

## Host controls

The person who scheduled the meeting (and anyone else given host status) has access to a controls panel to:

- Turn specific permissions on or off for non-hosts — screen sharing, chat, reactions, microphone, and camera.
- Mute everyone at once.
- End the meeting for all participants.

When a host mutes someone or changes what they can do, that person sees a brief, clear notice explaining what changed.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Common actions have keyboard shortcuts — toggling your microphone or camera, opening chat or the participant list, raising your hand, switching to fullscreen, and more. Press **?** during a meeting to see the full list.

## Recording

Meetings are recorded automatically. Before joining, everyone sees a notice that the call may be recorded for review. Staying in the call is treated as acknowledgment of this.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are meetings recorded?">
    Yes. Meetings are recorded automatically. Everyone sees a notice on the pre-join screen that the call may be recorded for review before they enter.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can a host mute or remove participants?">
    Hosts can mute any participant and turn off their camera. Hosts can also restrict what non-hosts are allowed to do — including screen sharing, chat, reactions, and microphone use. Ending the meeting removes everyone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I lose my connection during a meeting?">
    You can rejoin using the same meeting link. The meeting continues for everyone else while you reconnect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when multiple people share their screen?">
    When more than one person shares their screen, the meeting view focuses on one of them at a time. Hosts can manage who has screen-share permission from the host controls panel.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
