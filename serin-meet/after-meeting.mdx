## Ending a meeting

When a host ends the meeting, participants see a brief end screen and are redirected. If a recording was in progress, the host sees a short "finishing up" screen while the recording saves — keep the tab open until it completes.

## Recordings

The recording is attached to the candidate's application once processing is complete. It can be accessed from the application detail view by any member of the hiring team.

## AI-generated meeting summary

Once a recorded meeting is processed, an AI-generated summary of the conversation may be available alongside the recording. This gives reviewers a quick overview without needing to watch the full recording.

## Recruiter notes and rating

After the meeting ends, the recruiter can:

- **Rate the meeting** on a 1–10 scale.
- **Add notes** about the conversation.

Both the rating and notes are saved to the candidate's application and are visible to the rest of the hiring team for reference during the review process.

## FAQ
