# Why We Have Interview Guidelines

Serin aims to provide a fair and consistent interview experience for every candidate. These guidelines help you understand what is expected during your interview and how your session is used in the hiring process.

# What We Expect

During your Serin interview, we expect you to:

- **Attend the interview yourself** — The person who applied should be the person interviewed.
- **Stay focused** — Give the interview your full attention for the duration of the session.
- **Answer honestly** — Share your genuine experience, skills, and perspective.
- **Avoid interruptions** — Minimize distractions from other people, devices, or applications.
- **Complete the interview independently** — Rely on your own knowledge and experience when responding.

# Things to Avoid

To maintain fairness for all candidates, please avoid:

- Having someone else answer questions on your behalf
- Frequently switching away from the interview window
- Using unauthorized assistance during the session
- Interrupting or disrupting the interview repeatedly

<Note>
These guidelines exist to protect both candidates and employers. Following them helps ensure your performance reflects your true abilities.
</Note>

# Privacy

- **Interview data is used for evaluation** — Your session recording, transcript, and responses are used to assess your fit for the role.
- **Organizations control access** — The hiring organization decides who on their team can view your interview report and related materials.
- **Your data is not public** — Interview results are shared only within the organization's hiring workflow, not published publicly on Serin.

For questions about how a specific organization handles your data, contact them directly.

# Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use notes during the interview?">
    Brief reference notes may be acceptable for some roles, but relying heavily on prepared scripts undermines the purpose of the interview. When in doubt, answer from your own experience. If the organization has specific rules, they will communicate them to you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I take a break during the interview?">
    Short pauses while thinking are natural. However, Serin interviews are designed to be completed in one sitting. Plan enough uninterrupted time before you start. If you experience a technical issue, follow the on-screen guidance or contact the organization.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I restart the interview?">
    Generally, no. Once an interview session ends, it cannot be restarted on your own. If you believe your session was affected by a technical problem, contact the organization that invited you to discuss next steps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I switch devices mid-interview?">
    No. Complete your interview on a single device. Switching devices mid-session may interrupt your session and affect your evaluation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if someone enters the room during my interview?">
    Brief interruptions happen. Try to interview in a private space. Repeated or sustained interruptions may affect how your session is reviewed by the organization.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
