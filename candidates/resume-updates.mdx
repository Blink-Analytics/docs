Your resume helps Serin and the hiring organization understand your background. Keeping it accurate and up to date ensures you are evaluated on your most relevant experience.

# Uploading a Resume

### Supported formats

Upload your resume in a standard document format such as **PDF** or **DOCX**. If a file fails to upload, try exporting your resume as a PDF and uploading again.

### File size

Each resume file must be **10 MB or smaller**. If your file exceeds this limit, try compressing it or remove unnecessary content before uploading.

### Best practices

- Use a clear, readable layout with standard fonts
- Include relevant skills, projects, and work experience for the role
- Name your resume descriptively (e.g., "Software Engineer Resume") so you can identify it when applying to multiple jobs
- You can store up to **three resumes** (maximum **10 MB** each) on your Serin profile and choose which one to use per application.

# Updating a Resume

### Managing resumes in your profile

You can manage the resumes stored in your Serin profile at any time. Your profile can contain a maximum of **three resumes** simultaneously. There is no limit to how many times you can update resumes in your profile, provided you never exceed the three-resume limit.

### Choosing a resume for an application

When applying for a job, you must select one of the resumes stored in your profile. Review your selection carefully before submitting. Once your application is submitted, **the selected resume cannot be changed or resubmitted** for that application.

# Tips

- **Keep your resume current** — Make sure you submit your latest resume for an application.
- **Include relevant projects** — Highlight work that matches the job you are applying for.
- **Choose the right resume per job** — If you have multiple resumes on your profile, select the one most relevant to each role.

<Note>
You can store up to three resumes in your profile, with each resume limited to **10 MB**. Before submitting an application, carefully choose the resume you want to use, as it cannot be changed after submission.
</Note>
