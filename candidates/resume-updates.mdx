Your resume helps Serin and the hiring organization understand your background. Keeping it accurate and up to date ensures you are evaluated on your most relevant experience.

# Uploading a Resume

### Supported formats

Upload your resume in a standard document format such as **PDF** or **DOCX**. If a file fails to upload, try exporting your resume as a PDF and uploading again.

### File size

Keep your file size reasonable. Very large files may take longer to upload or fail validation. If you encounter an error, try compressing the file or removing unnecessary images.

### Best practices

- Use a clear, readable layout with standard fonts
- Include relevant skills, projects, and work experience for the role
- Name your resume descriptively (e.g., "Software Engineer Resume") so you can identify it when applying to multiple jobs
- You can store up to **three resumes** on your Serin profile and choose which one to use per application

# Updating a Resume

### When updates are allowed

You can update the resume on a specific job application **after applying but before starting your interview**. This is useful if you want to submit a version better tailored to the role.

You will see a message such as: *"You can change your resume before starting the interview."*

### Limits

- Maximum of **3 resume updates** per application
- Updates are **not available** after your interview has started or completed
- Updates may be blocked if your application is in certain statuses (for example, after screening outcomes that prevent further changes)

### When screening runs again

When you upload a new resume before your interview, Serin **re-scores** it against the job requirements. Your updated similarity score replaces the previous one. This may affect whether you proceed to the interview, depending on the organization's settings.

# Tips

- **Keep your resume current** — Update it before applying, not only before the interview.
- **Include relevant projects** — Highlight work that matches the job you are applying for.
- **Avoid heavy image layouts** — Text-based resumes parse more reliably than image-heavy designs.
- **Choose the right resume per job** — If you have multiple resumes on your profile, select the one most relevant to each role.

<Note>
Resume updates on a job application are separate from managing resumes on your profile. Profile resumes can be edited from your account settings. Application-level updates apply only to that specific job.
</Note>
