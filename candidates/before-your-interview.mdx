Serin uses an AI interviewer to conduct structured interviews for job roles. This guide walks you through what to expect, how to prepare, and what happens before, during, and after your session.

# Overview

When you receive an interview invitation, you will complete your application on Serin and then join a live AI-led interview from your browser. Serin evaluates your responses against the job's requirements and generates a report for the hiring organization.

### What to expect

- A conversational interview led by Serin's AI agent
- Questions tailored to your resume and the job requirements
- Additional organization-specific questions, if the employer has configured them
- A structured evaluation after your session ends

### Interview duration

Interview length depends on the role configuration. Sessions are typically between **20 and 60 minutes**. The on-screen timer shows how much time remains during your interview.

### AI interviewer

Serin's AI agent conducts the interview in a natural, conversational style. It asks questions based on your background and the job's objectives, and may follow up on your answers to understand your experience more deeply.

### Resume-based questions

The agent uses your uploaded resume to ask relevant questions about your skills, projects, and experience. Make sure your resume is up to date before you begin.

### Organization-specific questions

Some employers add custom questions for their roles. If configured, these appear as part of the normal interview flow — you do not need to prepare for them differently.

# Before You Begin

Use this checklist before starting your interview:

- Stable internet connection
- Quiet environment with minimal background noise
- Working webcam
- Working microphone
- Latest version of **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**
- Resume uploaded to your application
- Close unnecessary applications and browser tabs

<Note>
Complete your interview in one sitting. Set aside enough uninterrupted time before you begin.
</Note>

# Interview Flow

```
Invitation
    ↓
Application & Resume
    ↓
System Checks
    ↓
Interview Starts
    ↓
Answer Questions
    ↓
Interview Ends
    ↓
AI Evaluation
```

1. **Invitation** — You receive an email or apply to a job on Serin.
2. **Application & Resume** — You complete your application and confirm your resume.
3. **System Checks** — Serin verifies your camera, microphone, and network connection.
4. **Interview Starts** — The AI agent greets you and begins the interview.
5. **Answer Questions** — Respond naturally and follow on-screen instructions.
6. **Interview Ends** — Your session is recorded and submitted for evaluation.
7. **AI Evaluation** — Serin generates a structured report for the organization.

# During the Interview

- **Speak naturally** — Treat it like a conversation, not a test with right-or-wrong answers.
- **Take your time** — It is fine to pause briefly before answering a question.
- **Follow on-screen instructions** — Serin may guide you through setup steps, permissions, or fullscreen mode.
- **Complete in one sitting** — Plan to finish the interview without long breaks.

# After the Interview

- **Evaluation begins** — Serin processes your session and generates a report.
- **Organization reviews** — The hiring team reviews your interview results alongside other application materials.
- **Results shared** — Whether and when you receive feedback depends on the organization's process. Contact the employer directly if you have questions about your application status.
