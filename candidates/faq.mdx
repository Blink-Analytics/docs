Quick answers to common questions about preparing for, completing, and following up on your Serin interview.

# Before Interview

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long is the interview?">
    Interview length depends on the role. Sessions are typically between 20 and 60 minutes. The on-screen timer shows remaining time during your session.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which browser should I use?">
    Google Chrome is recommended. Microsoft Edge is also supported. See [Technical Requirements →](/candidates/technical-requirements) for full details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a camera?">
    Yes. A working webcam is required for interview setup and the live session.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to create a Serin account?">
    Yes. You need an account to apply, upload your resume, and complete your interview.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I update my resume before the interview?">
    Yes, in most cases. You can update your resume up to 3 times per application before starting the interview. See [Resume Updates →](/candidates/resume-updates) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# During Interview

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I pause the interview?">
    Brief pauses while thinking are fine, but interviews are designed to be completed in one sitting. Plan enough uninterrupted time before you begin.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if my internet disconnects?">
    If your connection drops, your session may be affected. Try to reconnect on the same device. If you cannot continue, contact the organization that invited you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I skip a question?">
    You can ask the agent to move on, but answering questions thoughtfully gives the organization a better picture of your abilities. Respond as completely as you can.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will the interview be in English?">
    Serin interviews are conducted in English. Answer in English for the best evaluation experience.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I have a technical issue during setup?">
    See [Technical Requirements →](/candidates/technical-requirements) for troubleshooting steps. Resolve camera, microphone, and network issues before starting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# After Interview

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When will I get my results?">
    Results are reviewed by the hiring organization. Timing varies by employer. Contact the organization directly for updates on your application status.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I retake the interview?">
    Generally, no. Retakes are at the organization's discretion. If you believe your session was affected by a technical issue, contact the employer to discuss options.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I download my interview report?">
    Interview reports are generated for the organization, not directly for candidates. Whether you receive feedback depends on the employer's process.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens after my interview ends?">
    Serin processes your session and generates a structured report. The organization reviews your results alongside other hiring materials and decides on next steps.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
