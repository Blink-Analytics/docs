## Scheduling controls

Recruiters can manage calls for a specific job from the job settings. Options include:

- **Start calls** — begin placing calls for new invitations on this job.
- **Stop calls** — pause calls for this job. Candidates invited while calls are paused will not be called.
- **Resume calls** — restart calls for this job. Only candidates invited after re-enabling will be called automatically.

These controls apply per job, not globally. Stopping calls on one job has no effect on others.

## Call timing

Calls are placed during reasonable daytime hours. The agent accounts for the candidate's location when scheduling call attempts, so a call is not placed at an unusual hour regardless of when the invitation was sent or when a recruiter triggered the action.

## Manual calls

A hiring team member can place a one-off call to a specific candidate at any time from the candidate's application. Manual calls follow the same agent behavior as automatic ones — the same introduction, same questions (if pre-screening is configured), same transcript logging.

## If a candidate opts out

Candidates can ask not to be called during the call itself. If a candidate says they do not want to be called, the agent ends the call politely and the application is updated so no further automatic calls are placed for that application.

## Privacy

Candidates are informed at the start of each call that:

- The call is being handled by an AI voice agent on behalf of the hiring team.
- The conversation may be recorded and transcribed for review by the hiring team.

This disclosure happens before any questions are asked. Continuing the call is treated as the candidate's acknowledgment.

Call transcripts and any associated data are handled according to Serin's [Privacy & AI Policy](https://www.serin-ai.com/privacy-ai-policy).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I turn off calls for all jobs at once?">
    Currently, calls are managed per job. To stop calls across all jobs, you would need to pause them on each job individually. Contact [connect@serin-ai.com](mailto:connect@serin-ai.com) if you need help with this.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to candidates who were invited while calls were paused?">
    They won't automatically get called when you resume. Pausing stops calls going forward — candidates invited during the pause are not queued and won't be caught up automatically. You'd need to place those calls manually from each candidate's application.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can a candidate request their call data be deleted?">
    Yes. Candidates can submit a data deletion request through Serin's privacy process. See the [Privacy & AI Policy](https://www.serin-ai.com/privacy-ai-policy) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are call transcripts visible to candidates?">
    No. Call transcripts are visible only to the hiring team members with access to that application.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
