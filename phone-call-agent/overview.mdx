The Serin Phone Call Agent is an AI voice assistant that calls candidates by phone on behalf of your hiring team. It handles two situations automatically: reminding a candidate about an interview they've been invited to, and running an initial phone pre-screening before the candidate moves forward.

No one on the hiring team needs to pick up a phone — the agent places calls automatically after a candidate is invited. A team member can also trigger a one-off call manually if needed.

## Two types of calls

Depending on how the job is set up, each application receives one of two types of calls:

| Call type | When it's used |
|---|---|
| **Reminder call** | The job has no pre-screening questions. The agent checks in to confirm the candidate received their invitation and intends to complete the interview. |
| **Phone pre-screening** | The job has pre-screening questions configured. The agent asks those questions over the phone before the candidate moves to the interview. |

Each application only ever receives one type of call. Calls are placed during reasonable daytime hours regardless of when the invitation was sent.

## When calls are placed

Calls are placed automatically after a candidate is invited. The timing follows reasonable daytime hours in the candidate's location. No call is placed if:

- The candidate's phone number is missing or invalid.
- The candidate has previously asked not to be called.
- The application's deadline has already passed.

A hiring team member can also place a manual one-off call from the candidate's application at any time.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to do anything to trigger a call?">
    No. The agent places the appropriate call automatically once a candidate is invited. If you need to place a one-off call manually, you can do that from the candidate's application.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I stop or pause calls for a specific job?">
    Yes. From the job's settings, you can turn reminder and pre-screening calls on or off for that job. Turning a type off stops those calls for everyone on the job going forward; turning it back on resumes them for new invitations. To manage a call for one specific candidate, use the controls on their application. See [Scheduling & Controls →](/phone-call-agent/scheduling-and-privacy) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What languages does the agent support?">
    Calls start in English. The agent also supports Hindi and can switch mid-conversation if the candidate responds in Hindi or asks to switch.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
